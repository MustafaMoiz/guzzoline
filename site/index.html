What it does

  • Usage

    How much of your session and your week you’ve used, and when each resets. It also shows if you’re using them up too fast.

  • Sessions

    Each Claude Code session gets a Clawd. Its color tells you what the session is doing: working, waiting for you, done, or done a while ago.

  • Sound

    Your Mac plays a sound when a session finishes or needs you. So you can walk away while Claude works.

  • Talking

    Hold a Clawd, say what you want, and let go. Guzzoline types it into that session and sends it.

  • Lid shut

    If you like, your Mac stays awake while a session works, even with the lid closed. So you can close it and go.

  • Privacy

    Guzzoline reads your usage and the titles of your sessions. It never reads your conversations.

Or let Claude Code install it

Paste this into Claude Code on your Mac, and it will do the rest.
